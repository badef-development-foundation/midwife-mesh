# Proposed pilot

**Location:** Villages and displacement camps around Afmadow and Dhobley, Lower Juba, Jubaland, Somalia.

**Planned users and endpoints:** 40 midwives; Waamo MCH; Bulakutuur Health Centre; four health posts; Kismayo Hospital.

**Estimated catchment:** 3,000 pregnant women annually, to be confirmed with the District Health Office in month 1.

**Approvals:** Jubaland Ministry of Health and relevant ethics/data governance reviews are planned, not represented as obtained.

**Monitoring:** Delivery rate, acknowledgement latency, monthly active midwives, retention, false alerts, downtime, missed acknowledgements and safeguarding incidents. No project results are yet available.
