# Problem statement and supporting evidence

In rural Afmadow, gaps in cellular coverage and emergency transport can delay referral of maternal and newborn complications. BADEF reports 548 skilled births and 206 emergency obstetric referrals in its 2022–2023 Afmadow health project. Its 2024–2025 Kismayo project reported midwife staffing challenges. These figures are prior BADEF programme results, not evidence that Midwife Mesh works.

**Hypothesis:** An affordable off-grid communications network, combined with reliable facility response procedures, could reduce delays between recognition of complications and referral coordination.

**To test:** network coverage, alert delivery, acknowledgement time, usability for low-literacy users, facility response, security and user safety.

Source: BADEF, *Midwife Mesh Concept Note*, November 2026, sections 1–2 and 4. Verify figures against underlying programme reports before public donor submission.
