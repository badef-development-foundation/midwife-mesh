# Proposed delivery partnership and governance

**BADEF:** Somali-led nonprofit; intended project lead, maternal health programme implementation, stakeholder engagement, safeguarding, monitoring and coordination with Jubaland health authorities.

**Aspire Graphics Ltd:** Proposed Kenyan-registered technology partner; intended technical design, software development, integration, testing, documentation and maintenance.

The concept note names Aspire Graphics as a proposed partner. This repository does not establish a signed contract, confirmed corporate eligibility, intellectual-property transfer or actual delivery of software. Confirm the partnership and publishing authority before adding proprietary materials.

**Planned long-term custodians:** Jubaland Ministry of Health and midwives' association, subject to formal agreement.
